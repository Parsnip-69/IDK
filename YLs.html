Young Leaders

Being a 4th YL

Even after bidding farewell to a scout when they reach a certain age, there's an exciting opportunity for them to continue their journey with the 4th. This is by becoming Young Leaders.

Young leaders actively engage with a younger section, assisting in weekly meetings and fostering essential friendships. This role, closer in age to the young people than adult leaders, creates a unique and impactful bond, enriching the scouting experience.

Young Leader Training

So to be a young leader, there is training that the young people can get involved in. This training contains 11 modules, which are the training areas that can either be done online or in person (Other then module A).

The modules are the training that build you up to do the 4 mission that are done in the young leader's section weekly meeting. Below is a photo the badges around the edge respresent the modules (Click each badge for more information).

Module A is the part of the young leader training that is compulsory. This is the module that teaches the young leader about their role and responibities. This module has to be done in person with the area's training advisor. At the end of the training, the young leader will be given a badge to show that they completed their training and an orange card (which has all the rules that the young leader must follow).





For more information about any of the module, click the badge in the middle above.

Explorer Units

So, we encourage our young leaders to go to an Explorer Unit so they have time to spend with people their own age and have less responibities. This also means that they get to do activities that are catered to a older age range and get to join in as when they are at explorers. They are the leader's top priority as they are the young people in this instance.



So although, 4th East Grinstead doesn't have a dedciated Explorers Unit. Within the East Grinstead District, there are 3 units.

  • Griffyn (East Grinstead)
  • Rowfant (Crawley Down)
  • Matterhorn (Copthorne)
