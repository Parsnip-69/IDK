Badges


Badges are the main focus with our programs/ schedule. This ensures that every youth member has the opportunity to earn as many as possible and get the learn/ understand what scouting has designed for the young people to learn external to the general education system. The badges represent the member's achievements and milestones within the movement.

Each Section's Badges

For the different section within the movement. There are different badges that they are able to earn. The main collections of badges are:

  • Activity Badges
  • Challenge Badges
  • Staged Activity Badges
    • Unlike the other two. Staged Activity Badges can be earned at any point as a youth member
