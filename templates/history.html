June 1954

The 4th East Grinstead Scout Group was orginally opened in June 1954.

Mid 1970s

Sometime during the mid-1970s, the group was closed. Unfortunately, due to very limited documentation from this period, the exact reasons remain unclear.

March 1981

Luckily for the group today, a local gentleman, Peter Roberts, reopened the group and the scout section.

July 1981

A few months later, the cubs section was opened.

May 1984
