June 1954 - Mid 70s

The 4th East Grinstead Scout Group was orginally opened in June 1954 and closed in the mid 70s. Unfortunately, we have very limited documentation between these periods.

March 1981

Luckily for the group today, a local gentleman, Peter Roberts, reopened the group and the scout section.

July 1981

A few months later, the cubs section was opened.

May 1984
