Documents


Below are the relevant documents for our group. These documents include our behaviour policy, the start pack and a draft of the minutes of our most recent AGM. We maintain all documents below at their most current versions. Soon to be added to on this page will be our risk assessments.

We have created a document that contains all the information necessary for new starters; which includes information regarding meeting times, the section leaders, safety information, and uniforms.

Our group's behavior policy, which outlines expected conduct for your young person during regular meetings, events, and camps. This is a rarely referred to document but is a necessary produre for our leaders to follow and for parents/ guardians to know about.
